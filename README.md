# Receipt Splitter

Split restaurant bills fairly among friends. No more "you had the steak, I had salad" arguments.

## What It Does

- Add people in your group
- Add receipt items with prices
- Assign each item to who ate it
- Calculates who owes what
- Shows grand total

## How to Use

1. **Add people** - Enter names one by one
2. **Add items** - Enter food name, price, select who ate it
3. **Calculate** - Tap button to see split totals
